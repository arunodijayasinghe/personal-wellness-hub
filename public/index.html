Wellness Tips

Simple guidance for each category, with its source.

🏃 Exercise

Adults are advised to do 150 to 300 minutes of moderate activity each week, which is about 30 minutes on most days.

Source: World Health Organization, physical activity guidelines

😴 Sleep

Most adults need 7 or more hours of sleep each night. Keep a regular bedtime and avoid screens before sleep.

Source: CDC, sleep and sleep disorders

💧 Water

About 8 glasses a day is a common rule of thumb, but your needs change with weather, activity and body size.

Source: Mayo Clinic, how much water to drink daily

🧘 Meditation

Even 5 to 10 minutes of slow breathing or mindfulness can help you feel calmer.

Source: NCCIH, meditation and mindfulness

😊 Mood

Talking to someone you trust, spending time outdoors and keeping a routine can support your mood. If low mood lasts for weeks, speak to a doctor or counsellor.

Source: World Health Organization, mental health

⚠️ General guidance only, not medical advice.
